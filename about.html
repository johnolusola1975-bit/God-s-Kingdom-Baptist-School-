About the school

Purposeful education. Strong values.

God's Kingdom Baptist School is a Nursery, Primary and Junior Secondary School located in Mowo, Badagry, Lagos.

School building / campus photo

Our motto: Better Mindset Is The Goal

We believe education should help a child develop knowledge, character, confidence and a healthy approach to learning. Our school is designed to provide a supportive environment where children can build strong foundations for the future.

✓Respect for every learner.
✓Encouragement of responsible behaviour.
✓Balanced academic and personal development.
✓Positive engagement with parents and guardians.
Our direction

Mission & vision

MISSION

To nurture learners

To provide a supportive educational environment where children develop useful knowledge, positive character and a mindset for lifelong learning.

VISION

Confident, responsible learners

To raise young people who are academically grounded, morally responsible and prepared to contribute positively to their communities.

COMMITMENT

School & home partnership

To maintain open, respectful communication with parents and guardians in support of each learner's development.
